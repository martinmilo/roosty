book-workshop   Book a place on a workshop                              Bookings
Reserve a place, take payment and confirm by email.

  entry    http POST /bookings
           app/controllers/bookings_controller.rb
  entry    mail BookingMailer#confirmation
           app/mailers/booking_mailer.rb
  code     app/models/booking.rb           holds the state machine
           app/services/book_workshop.rb
  try      Open /workshops and pick a date with free places.
           Book with card 4242 4242 4242 4242 and any future expiry.
           Check the confirmation mail in /letter_opener.

OUTCOMES
  confirmed   payment succeeds: the booking is saved, the place count goes down
              and the confirmation mail is sent
  full        no places left: booking refused, nothing saved

WHY
  Payment is taken before the place is held so that abandoned checkouts never
  block a seat.

  Confirmation goes by mail rather than SMS because most bookings come from the
  studio's newsletter.
